# Contributing to Race Crew Planner

Thank you for helping improve Race Crew Planner. Please read [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md)
and [the worktree playbook](docs/agent-worktrees.md) before opening a pull
request. In particular, use a focused branch and worktree, keep secrets out of
the repository, and provide verification evidence.

Do not submit material whose license, employer terms, or third-party rights do
not permit this grant. If you contribute on behalf of an employer or other
organization, ensure you have its authorization first.

## Pull requests

Open PRs against `dev` using the repository template. Describe the purpose and
scope, list commands run and their results, and call out effects on
authentication, data, analytics, or infrastructure. Maintainers may ask for a
separate contributor agreement before accepting substantial contributions.

For security issues, do not open a public issue; follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
