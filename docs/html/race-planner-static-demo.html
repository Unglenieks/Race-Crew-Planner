Prototype review · v1 → v2

What changed in v2

The previous prototype demonstrated the feature surface but broke several of the rules the specification sets. These are the corrections, each visible somewhere in this file.

Guardrail E · dead controls42 of 168 controls did nothing at all25 overflow menus and 17 toolbar buttons had no behaviour and gave no feedback, so a reviewer could not tell a defect from an unbuilt feature. Every ⋯ now opens a real menu, and every control acts or explains itself.
Guardrail F · false claimsThe interface stated things that were not trueThe sidebar advertised 25 screens over 24. The Saturday tab claimed 6 movements and listed 3. Counts are now generated from the content, and every day tab lists what it promises.
Principle 03 · state must be explicitMobile hid the status column and the primary actionThe old layout removed the third and fourth cell of every row under 850px, which deleted the status tag on the plan and the Open button in the records list. Rows now wrap instead of truncating, so state and action survive every width.
§12 · type and contrastType ran down to 8px at under 3:1 contrastTags, labels, metadata and the whole sidebar sat between 8 and 10px in light grey — unreadable in sun, in gloves, or with average eyesight. Body is now 15px with a 12px secondary floor and an 11px absolute floor for labels.
§12 · targets and focus16px tap targets and no focus styling anywhereTick boxes and overflow menus were 16px, well under a gloved fingertip, and no element had a focus ring, so keyboard users were invisible to themselves. Targets are now 36px minimum, 44px on touch, with a visible focus ring on everything.
§07 · blocked submissionA blocked submit was announced in a 3.6s toastThe reason someone could not finish disappeared before they could read it, and no field was marked. Validation is now in place, per field, with focus moved to the first problem and the message persisting until resolved.
§09 · deliveryPublishing reported success to the senderThe old flow said “Published. Only affected people were notified,” which the product could not know. Publishing now reports reach — 6 of 8 — names who is unreached, and offers escalation and radio acknowledgement.
Guardrail A · undoEverything was confirmed, nothing could be undoneTicking a checklist item now applies immediately with an undo, while merge, permanent delete and access removal state their specific impact before committing. Confirmation is reserved for what cannot be reversed.
§03 · viewsDay tabs, checklist tabs and activity filters were decorativeAll three now switch real content, follow tablist keyboard conventions, and the plan filter produces a genuine empty state naming the filter that caused it.
§02 · attention and navigationThe attention indicator had nowhere to goA badge with no destination sends people back to rebuild a filter. There is now an attention queue, Today’s counts open the list behind them, and a detail screen keeps its parent module selected.
§12 · assistive technologyNo labels, no dialog semantics, no announcementsNot one input was associated with its label, dialogs did not trap or return focus, and status messages were invisible to a screen reader. All of that is now wired, and the page title changes per screen so history is legible.
§12 · night useOne bright palette for a sport that runs after darkA white screen in a dark car damages the night vision of the person holding it and the driver beside them. Night mode is in the top bar and follows the device setting by default.
§10 and §11b · missing statesEmpty and failure states were never drawnThe old file styled an empty state and never used it, and covered only offline as a failure. First run, runtime empty, load failure, permission denial, stale views, failed publish, partial download and revoked access are now real screens.
Still deliberately missingTimeline, calendar, board and map are named but not drawn — they are build-next in the specification, and drawing them here would imply decisions nobody has made yet. The three open questions in §14 of the spec are also unanswered on purpose.
